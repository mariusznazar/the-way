# Research of the starting set

Give each standing check to a fresh agent of its own, all at once, with the technology, the
preferences, the scope being added where this is an extension, and the binding sentences of the
decisions that settle it: the tools and their consequences for the product, the sets of rules a
linter offers with the rules its documentation marks as recommended, and the advisory databases an
audit reads. Each source is a page with its version, never memory and never a fixed list; each
report goes to `.runs/coding/<question>.md`, the question's name in lower case with hyphens.

Once the reports are in, give one fresh agent, following `critique`, each report with its sources,
and ask it to refute every claim no source supports — a tool's use, a rule, a difference between
tools. Before the first critique of the session, read `elements/common/critique/SKILL.md`. Verify
each observation and give your verdict, correct the report by the verdicts, then end it with the
line `critique: passed`. Where no fresh agent can run, the research does not go on: stop and say so
(WAY-211, WAY-120). What the research finds is proposed in step 4, never imposed.
