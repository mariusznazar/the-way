# Hand back

On the route of one kind, check only what "The route of one kind" in `SKILL.md` gives, of the
items below. For a new kind in a product that has coding guidelines, check of the standing checks
only those the kind touches, as "A new kind" in step 3 of `SKILL.md` gives.

- Check that the task document holds what `task_document.md` beside this skill names, that each
  standing check has its adopting or departing decision among the choices settled (WAY-202), that
  a task of the first guidelines, of an extension or of a refresh that met the check of
  `recognize.md`, where `guidelines/coding.md` did not yet hold the rules on failures and logs, has
  the product's decision on them among the choices settled, adopted or departed from with its reason
  (WAY-279), and that no choice stands settled without the human's words. A task that found those
  rules in place, a heading holding `none —` among them, checks nothing of them. The same holds
  for the product's decision on the rule of size, which a task of the first guidelines, of an
  extension or of a refresh that met the check of `recognize.md` has among the choices settled,
  adopted with its thresholds and exceptions or departed from with its reason (WAY-355).
- Where step 3's audit ran, check what "Before handing back" in `product-guidelines/audit_end.md`
  gives.
- Where the human answered yes to a backlog task for a deferred `grave` cause, follow "A deferred
  cause of an audit" in `project-level/task/skill/guideline_findings.md` before handing back
  (WAY-284).
- In a task that carries the testing guidelines too (WAY-247), hand back to `refine-task` step 3,
  not to its step 4, while `maintain-testing-guidelines` has not led its matters in this task, also
  where it has no audit, as on a new level of tests. Where the audit stopped for the testing skill's,
  its list holds no order and no decision yet. That step names no skill of a discipline, so this
  line is what starts the next one: there, `maintain-testing-guidelines` leads its matters, and its
  audit, where it has one and it runs last, ends the list. Only the skill that leads last hands back
  to step 4.
- Where the audit stopped and the human has since separated the work, so that the testing skill's
  audit will not run in this task, return here: close the list as the audit that runs last does, as
  `product-guidelines/audit_end.md` gives, and then hand back to `refine-task` step 4.
- Otherwise hand back to `refine-task` step 4. Its step 5 finds the rows of *Settlements to record*
  through `find-foundation-entries` in task mode, with the task document, this conversation and any
  research reports and the trial-run reports as its source, and its step 6 asks the human to accept
  the whole document.
