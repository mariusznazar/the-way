# Starting set

Before the first test, for tests that have no testing guidelines yet, and at every refresh or
extension (WAY-244, WAY-249):

- Ask, as one question of assessment, for the human's preferences for the product's tests in the
  technology that the product's foundation settles — which levels it has, where their files lie,
  what a test replaces and with what; "I do not know" is an answer. Word it as "How to show" in
  `SKILL.md` gives. A tool the human prefers is the one proposed, and no other is offered in its
  place (WAY-249).
- For the first testing guidelines where the human has no preference, and at every extension,
  research the set for that technology: read `research.md` beside this skill. At a refresh, do no
  research and use the accepted guidelines with the update or correction the task calls for.
