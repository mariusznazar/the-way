# Research of the basic rules

Split research into the questions the starting set or the scope being added must answer there —
how the technology names and pairs colour roles, how it draws focus, how its controls show their
states — and give each to a fresh agent of its own, all at once, with the technology, the
preferences, the scope being added where this is an extension, and the binding sentences of the
decisions that settle it. Include any new tool's consequences for the product. Each source is a
page with its version, never memory and never a fixed list; each report goes to
`.runs/styleguide/<part>/<question>.md`, the question's name in lower case with hyphens.

Once the reports are in, give one fresh agent, following `critique`, each report with its sources,
and ask it to refute every claim no source supports — a rule, or a difference between ways of
following one. Before the first critique of the session, read `elements/common/critique/SKILL.md`.
Verify each observation and give your verdict, correct the report by the verdicts, then end it
with the line `critique: passed`. Where no fresh agent can run, the research does not go on: stop
and say so (WAY-186, WAY-120). What the research finds is proposed in step 4, never imposed.
