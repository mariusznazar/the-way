# Glossary

The glossary gives each word a product's rules stand on one meaning, so that the same word does not
mean one thing in a decision, another in an invariant and a third in the code. The glossary of
the-way holds the words of the process; a product's glossary holds the words of its domain and takes
the words of the process from the-way at the commit it pins (WAY-92).

An entry holds its term, one definition and two fields: *Avoid*, other words for what the term names,
and *Not*, other meanings the word carries in the foundation (WAY-94). Nothing cites a term by an
identifier or a fingerprint: a sentence depends on a term by using its word, and a changed definition
goes through a review of the term's uses (WAY-97).

This description grows into the element's template as the element goes through its steps.
`template.md` holds the rules of an entry; the skill that writes entries and the guard that checks
them come in the element's next steps.
