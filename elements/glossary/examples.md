# Glossary entry examples

Verbatim copies of entries from this repository's glossary (`foundations/glossary.md`), chosen to
show each form the template describes: `invariant`, with both fields `none`; `gate`, whose *Avoid*
holds another word for what the term names; `foundation`, whose *Not* names a meaning the word no
longer carries, with the quoted word that carries it now. An entry carries no version and is edited
in place, so a copy follows its entry, and the format guard checks that every example matches its
entry word for word.

## invariant

An invariant is a sentence about the product that is true or false on every commit.

- **Avoid:** none
- **Not:** none

## gate

The gate is the program that runs the checks a change calls for.

- **Avoid:** quality gate — same thing
- **Not:** none

## foundation

A product's foundation is the written record of the rules the product holds itself to, of what those rules stand on and of where the product breaks them.

- **Avoid:** none
- **Not:** the files of `foundations/` taken together with the templates of the elements — "elements' templates"
