# Part map entry examples

The entries of this repository's own part map, `foundations/part_map.md`, that show the form of an
entry, named by identifier, one for each kind of entry `template.md` describes. Each is read there,
in the commit of the-way a product pins, never copied here. Until the foundation is migrated, these
entries keep the earlier form the template's last sections describe; read a part for its sentence,
*Files*, *Depends on* and *Trust boundary*, and a surface for its promise.

- `decisions` — a part that depends on other parts
- `repository` — a part that depends on none
- `entry-format` — a surface: what the-way promises the products that pin it
