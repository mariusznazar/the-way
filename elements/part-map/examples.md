# Part map entry examples

The entries of this repository's own part map, `foundations/part_map.md`, that show the form of an
entry, named by identifier, one for each kind of entry `template.md` describes. Each is read there,
in the commit of the-way a product pins, never copied here.

- `part-map` — a part, with every field filled
- `task-cycle` — a part whose tool does not exist yet, so it holds no files
- `wording-reaches-dependants` — a critical flow
- `content-fingerprint` — a surface declared at the item itself
- `entry-format` — a surface that cannot be marked at the item, and leans on a test instead
