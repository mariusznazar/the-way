# Read

- Select and read facts and their actual decision and invariant dependents by "Reading the
  foundation" in `elements/common/way_of_working.md`, using the complete part map where it exists.
- A product that starts its facts here writes every fact an invariant's *Rests on* or a decision's
  *Based on* waits for (WAY-90) — one reaching a pending line going to the human first.
- A change that brings what entries of the foundation wait for also fixes every entry that waits
  for it, in the same change. An entry that says what holds until something exists turns false when
  it arrives, and nothing leads from what arrives to what waits for it, so a fix left for later goes
  missing.
