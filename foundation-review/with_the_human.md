# Going through the list with the human

Read at step 6 of `SKILL.md`, after the critique. Ask as `elements/common/way_of_working.md` gives
under "How a skill asks": in plain text, with the ways open, what each costs and a recommendation.
Show the material the human is to decide on and one question about it, never a summary of the
analysis.

## The order

1. The proposals about the foundation, **one at a time**. Show the entry's words that no longer
   hold, quoted whole; what the evidence shows; what happens if it stays as it is and what the
   change costs; your recommendation; and one question — change, leave, or check first. Wait for
   the answer before the next proposal. An answer that changes the shape of later proposals changes
   them.
2. The groups of remarks, **a group at a time**. Show the group's kind, its count, one remark in
   full — the reviewer's words, the place, the state today and what it does to whom — and the
   effect of the rest in one sentence; the options (a collective task, a note to a task, leave),
   what each costs, your recommendation; and one question. Show the full table of the group only
   when the human asks for it.
3. The collective task for small corrections of the foundation's wording, if there is one, last.

Write each decision under its proposal or group in the data file when it is made.

## The tasks that follow

A decision to change something becomes a task in the backlog step 1 found, and the review makes
the task and no change itself (WAY-302, WAY-14):

- Create the task in the form the backlog gives: an entry, and where the backlog holds short
  entries only, a task document in the task area, linked from the entry, with `Status: refining`
  and `Project relation: independent` as `project-level/task/template.md` gives, its *Need*
  taken from the proposal or the group and pointing at the section of the data file; the rest is
  left to `refine-task`, which refines the task before any cycle takes it.
- A note to an existing task is added to that task's document, with the number of remarks and the
  section of the data file.
- Where the agent cannot write to the backlog, give the human the text of each task and note, and
  ask them to place it. A task or a note the human declines is not made, and the data file says so.

A proposal to change a decision changes nothing in the log: the task that carries it is refined,
and its cycle writes the change, which the human accepts by merging its request (WAY-14).
