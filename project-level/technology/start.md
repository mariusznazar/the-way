- Read the definition whole, `project-level/definition_template.md` in full, and the section "How a
  skill asks" of `elements/common/way_of_working.md`.
- Where "Projected foundation changes" reads `TODO` or holds its working table, the step that finds
  entries has not ended: return to `define-project` step 5.
- Select and read the product's current foundation by "Reading the foundation" in
  `elements/common/way_of_working.md` (WAY-150). Include every current decision that holds or
  constrains a listed technology choice; refresh the selection as the list takes shape.
- Where "Technology" reads `TODO`, begin with the step "List the choices" (`list.md` beside this file).
- Where it holds a list, first take the look at the logs, the interface and the development data
  that `running_product.md` beside this file gives, and the look at how a human learns of a failure
  that `failure_notice.md` beside this file gives. Add each matter they find missing as an item
  ending `— open`; end `— open` each item of the three matters whose entry, held or projected, the
  first look finds silent on part of what the matter asks, with that entry nested under it. Where a
  look adds or changes an item, show the list again as the step "List the choices" does (`list.md`
  beside this file) before going on, with one sentence for each changed item saying what the entry
  named leaves unsaid (WAY-121, WAY-165). A definition the gate has approved does not come back to
  this step for these matters or for the failure matter.
- Where it holds an item ending `— open`, look first under "Projected foundation changes" for a
  decision written for that choice that answers everything the template asks of the choice, so that
  a silent one nested under the item does not count; where one stands, end the item
  `— projected: <its question>`. A step resumed after an interrupted write thus finds the rewritten
  decision.
  Then show the list and ask whether it still holds; where the human adds or changes an item,
  follow the step "List the choices" (`list.md` beside this file) for it. Then resume the step
  "Settle the open choices" (`settle.md` beside this file) with the open items and the preferences written
  under them; a report of an open choice under `.runs/technology/` that reads `critique: passed`
  and answers what the template asks of the choice is reused, and the research of the others starts
  again.
- Where it holds a list with no open item, go to the step "Close" (`close.md` beside this file).
