# The look at how a human learns of a failure

The look of the skill `settle-technology` at how a human learns of a failure of the running
product, whose `SKILL.md` lies beside this file. The step "List the choices" takes it for a new
list, and the step "Start or resume" for a list already written.

The look applies unless the definition, or a decision of the foundation, keeps the product on the
machine on which it is made. A definition that says the product goes to a server meets it, and so
does a definition silent on where the product runs; the condition reads what the definition and
the foundation say, never a marker of the list, which has none yet when the look first runs
(WAY-270).

Where it applies, look for a decision of the foundation that settles the matter, a deliberate
absence included: where one stands, the matter is not listed. Otherwise list it as a choice of its
own, ending `— open`, even where its answer adds nothing to run, pay for or back up. It is no
matter of an agent's way to the running product: `running_product.md` does not apply to it, and
whether an agent can read an option is a consequence like any other. A matter listed is settled
like any other choice from here on, and a deliberate absence always enters its comparison, as the
template's Technology section gives (WAY-270).

A list that has the matter already needs nothing from this look. A definition the gate has approved
does not come back to the step for it.
