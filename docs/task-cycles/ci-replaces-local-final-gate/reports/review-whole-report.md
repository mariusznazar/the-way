### I-1. The committed record copy never holds the CI final gate, so the next cycle's step 4 loses its list of failing tests and runs a full local gate on its base every time

- Place: `task-cycle/skill/SKILL.md:159-171` (step 4) against `task-cycle/skill/SKILL.md:613-621` (step 12) and `task-cycle/record_template.md:149-159`; commit `b646084`.
- What is wrong: step 12 writes the CI run's result into *Verification* of the **original** record only after the record's pair of commits is pushed, and says: "The committed copy of the record holds that row only for the runs before its own head: the run on the head that removes the copy comes after it, and no pair of commits is repeated to bring that row in." When CI is the final gate, the cycle also runs no local gate at step 10. So the copy `docs/task-cycles/<slug>/cycle.md` that the branch adds holds no result of its final gate at all. The only exception is the red runs on earlier heads, and those are not the final tree. Step 4 of the next cycle takes "the list of the tests failing before the change from the final gate of the cycle of the merged branch ... from the merged branch's own copy of its cycle record". The change added "the CI run's result of step 12 included, with each check that failed named". That sentence says the copy holds something that step 12 of the same file says it does not hold. Step 4's own fallback then applies: "or the merged branch holds no copy of a cycle record with the result of its final gate, run the checks on the base". Two more gaps follow:
  - the CI row holds "each check by name and its conclusion", which means the jobs `tests` and `tests-windows`, not the names of failing tests;
  - `request_template.md:485-487` ("the list of failing tests it took from the earlier cycle's final gate") inherits the same gap.
- Why it matters: in the usual case the base of a cycle is the merge of the previous cycle's branch. Step 4 skips the base run in that case so that "a full run of the gate" is not added "to the wait of every cycle" (`SKILL.md:159-160`). After this change, every cycle that follows a cycle whose final gate was CI runs a full local gate on its base. The local full run is not removed, only moved from the end of one cycle to the start of the next. That defeats the task's *Need*: "testy biegną dwa razy, a komputer jest zajęty przez czas lokalnego przebiegu". It also breaks the premise of the answer to "Które przebiegi pełnej bramki przechodzą do CI?", which says the base run stays local because "oba zdarzają się rzadko". An orchestrator that reads only the changed sentence, and not the fallback, would instead look for a CI result in a copy that has none.
- Evidence: `SKILL.md:166-167` ("holds the final gate's result in its *Verification*, the CI run's result of step 12 included") against `SKILL.md:619-621` and `record_template.md:157-159` ("The committed copy holds the row of the CI run only for the runs before its own head"). The orchestrator knows at step 10 that CI will be the final gate, before the copy is made, but nothing writes that into the copy, and nothing gives step 4 another source for the result (for example the merged request's *Checks*, which step 12 edits after the run, or the run on the merged head read through the host's tool).
- Within the task: To do 1, the base run of step 4 staying local and rare, and the *Need*.
