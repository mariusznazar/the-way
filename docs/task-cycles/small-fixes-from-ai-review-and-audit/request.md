## What you will see

Nine small improvements to how the process works, all from the review of the AI layer and the audit of the process:

- **The weekly audit shows whole series, and compares before and after a fix.** Until now the audit showed its figures one version of the process at a time, and a version often has a single cycle, so two audits in a row put the decision off. - **The gate's timing script stops inventing directories.** When a test of the gate itself fails and prints the output of a nested run of the gate, the script took the nested directories for real ones, wrote directories that do not exist and gave the failing directory somebody else's time.
