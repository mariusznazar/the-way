# Archived cycle evidence

Stage handoffs and reports are retained in full, except redundant complete Git-held source quotations replaced by exact commit/path references in foundation.md. Copied source selections, transient diffs, helper scripts, private acceptance fixtures and disposable repositories are excluded. The first ANSI-encoded final-fix RED log is represented by the faithful UTF-8 repeat final-fix-red-utf8-log.txt and the author report; UTF-16 PowerShell logs are losslessly transcoded to UTF-8. Commands and raw historical paths keep their actual invocation text; the links below locate the retained evidence.
